My colleague, James, has just been promoted to Senior Account Manager and will be starting his new role at Deloitte next quarter. I want to buy him a tablet as a congratulatory gift and need help deciding between three options: iPad Pro (M4), Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra, and Microsoft Surface Pro 11.
Objective Create a comprehensive, visually appealing slide deck that compares these three devices specifically for a corporate professional's use. The presentation should be polished and persuasive rather than dry or overly technical, helping both my colleague and me make an informed decision.
Target Audience Primary: Newly promoted corporate professional entering a client-facing role Secondary: Colleague making the purchase decision
Design Requirements Theme: Use Deloitte's official colors (Deloitte Green and black) throughout the presentation Tone: Professional, confident, and easy to understand Focus: Business professional needs and workplace lifestyle
Slide Structure and Content
Slide 1: Title Slide
Title (bold): "A Gift for James!"
Subtitle: "A Comparison of the iPad Pro (M4), Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra, and Microsoft Surface Pro 11"
Image: Photo representing Deloitte (office or branding scene)
The title and subtitle are to the left, and the image is to the right of the slide
Slide 2: The Challenge & The Goal
Explain the decision-making challenge
Define what we're trying to achieve
Slide 3: How We'll Judge (The Criteria)
List evaluation criteria relevant to business professionals:
Client presentation capability
Battery life for long workdays and travel
Portability and weight for commuting
Performance for productivity and multitasking
Security and enterprise compatibility
Other relevant factors
Slides 4-6: Deep Dive on Each Device
One slide per device (iPad Pro M4, Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra, Microsoft Surface Pro 11) Content per slide:
Key features and specifications
Pros and cons with respect to professional/corporate use
Two product images showing different angles
All the information should come from the official website of each option. Cite the resources when needed.
Slide 7: Side-by-Side Comparison
Format: Three-column comparison table, with devices as column headers
Content: Feature-by-feature comparison using the established criteria
Color coding system:
Green: Best performer in each category
Yellow: Middle performer
Red: Lowest performer in that category
Slide 8: Who Is Each Device For? (The Recommendation)
Summarize information on each option and match each device to different professional types/needs
Provide clear recommendations based on different priorities
Additional Guidelines
Keep technical jargon to a minimum
Use business-relatable examples and scenarios
Include practical considerations for corporate life (travel, client meetings, remote work)
Ensure all information is current and accurate
Make the presentation visually engaging with appropriate use of images, colors, and formatting
