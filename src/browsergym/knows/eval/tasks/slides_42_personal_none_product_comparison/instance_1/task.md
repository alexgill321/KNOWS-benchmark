My sister, Kathy, has graduated from high school and will be attending the University of Utah in a few months. I want to buy her an laptop as a graduation gift and need help deciding between three options: M4 MacBook Air, Lenovo Yoga 9i Aura Edition, and Surface Laptop.

Objective
Create a comprehensive, visually appealing slide deck that compares these three devices specifically for college student use. The presentation should be engaging and supportive rather than dry or overly technical, helping both my sister and me make an informed decision.

Target Audience
Primary: Recent high school graduate heading to college
Secondary: Family member making the purchase decision

Design Requirements
Theme: Use the University of Utah's official colors throughout the presentation
Tone: Exciting, supportive, and easy to understand
Focus: College student needs and lifestyle

Slide Structure and Content
Slide 1: Title Slide

Title (bold): "A Gift for Kathy!"
Subtitle: "A Comparison of the M4 MacBook Air, Lenovo Yoga 9i Aura Edition, and Surface Laptop"
Image: Photo representing the University of Utah (campus scene)
The title and subtitle are to the left, and the image is to the right of the slide

Slide 2: The Challenge & The Goal

Explain the decision-making challenge
Define what we're trying to achieve

Slide 3: How We'll Judge (The Criteria)

List evaluation criteria relevant to college students:

Durability for campus life
Battery life
Portability
Performance for academic tasks
Budget considerations
Other relevant factors

Slides 4-6: Deep Dive on Each Device

One slide per device ([Option1], [Option2], [Option3])
Content per slide:
Key features and specifications
Pros and cons, w.r.t. college use
Two product images showing different angles

All the information should come from the official website of the option. Cite the resources when needed.

Slide 7: Side-by-Side Comparison

Format: Three-column comparison table, with devices as column headers
Content: Feature-by-feature comparison using the established criteria
Color coding system:
Green: Best performer in each category
Yellow: Middle performer
Red: Lowest performer in that category

Slide 8: Who Is Each Device For? (The Recommendation)

Summarize information on each option and match each device to different student types/needs
Provide clear recommendations based on different priorities

Additional Guidelines
Keep technical jargon to a minimum
Use student-relatable examples and scenarios
Include practical considerations for college life
Ensure all information is current and accurate
Make the presentation visually engaging with appropriate use of images, colors, and formatting
