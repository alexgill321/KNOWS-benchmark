My student, Maya, has just been accepted into the PhD program in Biochemistry and will be starting her graduate studies at Johns Hopkins University this fall. I want to buy her a scientific notebook/lab journal as a welcome gift and need help deciding between three options: Leuchtturm1917 Hardcover Notebook, Rhodia Webnotebook, and Moleskine Classic Hard Cover Notebook.
Objective Create a comprehensive, visually appealing slide deck that compares these three notebooks specifically for a graduate researcher's use. The presentation should be encouraging and practical rather than dry or overly technical, helping both my student and me make an informed decision.
Target Audience Primary: Incoming PhD student entering a laboratory research environment Secondary: Academic advisor making the purchase decision
Design Requirements Theme: Use Johns Hopkins University's official colors (Heritage Blue and black) throughout the presentation Tone: Warm, encouraging, and easy to understand Focus: Graduate student research needs and academic lifestyle
Slide Structure and Content
Slide 1: Title Slide
Title (bold): "A Gift for Maya!"
Subtitle: "A Comparison of the Leuchtturm1917 Hardcover Notebook, Rhodia Webnotebook, and Moleskine Classic Hard Cover Notebook"
Image: Photo representing Johns Hopkins University (campus or lab scene)
The title and subtitle are to the left, and the image is to the right of the slide
Slide 2: The Challenge & The Goal
Explain the decision-making challenge
Define what we're trying to achieve
Slide 3: How We'll Judge (The Criteria)
List evaluation criteria relevant to graduate researchers:
Paper quality for ink and gel pen compatibility
Durability for lab and field environments
Page layout options (dotted, lined, blank) for notes and sketches
Indexing and organization features
Size and portability for carrying between lab, library, and class
Other relevant factors
Slides 4-6: Deep Dive on Each Notebook
One slide per product (Leuchtturm1917, Rhodia Webnotebook, Moleskine Classic) Content per slide:
Key features and specifications
Pros and cons with respect to graduate research use
Two product images showing different angles
All the information should come from the official website of each option. Cite the resources when needed.
Slide 7: Side-by-Side Comparison
Format: Three-column comparison table, with notebooks as column headers
Content: Feature-by-feature comparison using the established criteria
Color coding system:
Green: Best performer in each category
Yellow: Middle performer
Red: Lowest performer in that category
Slide 8: Who Is Each Notebook For? (The Recommendation)
Summarize information on each option and match each notebook to different researcher types/needs
Provide clear recommendations based on different priorities
Additional Guidelines
Keep technical jargon to a minimum
Use researcher-relatable examples and scenarios (lab records, lecture notes, field sketches, literature reviews)
Include practical considerations for graduate academic life
Ensure all information is current and accurate
Make the presentation visually engaging with appropriate use of images, colors, and formatting.
