# Checkpoints
This task has 37 points in total.

## Checkpoint 1 (2 pt):
5 different papers were looked at and placed into the google docs.

### Outcome Evaluation:
- Paper website was accessed at least 5 times for different papers.
- Links in the google docs lines up with the pages that were accessed.

## Checkpoint 2 (10 pt):
The papers meet the requirements for citation counts and recency.

### Outcome Evaluation:
- Each paper has at least 20 citations (1pt each).
- Each paper is from the last 5 years (1pt each).

## Checkpoint 3 (20 pt):
The doc structure for each paper is correct.

### Outcome Evaluation:
- Each paper abstract is included in the google docs.
- Each paper title is included in the google docs.
- Each paper link is included in the google docs.
- Each paper structure is correct: Title -> Link -> Abstract.

## Checkpoint 4 (5 pt):
The papers are from the correct relevant domain.

### Outcome Evaluation:
- LLM as Judge for the relevance of each paper abstract to quantum error correction (QEC).
