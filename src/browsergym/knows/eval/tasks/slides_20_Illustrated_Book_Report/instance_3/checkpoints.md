# Checkpoints
This task has 35 points in total.

## Checkpoint 1 (6pt):
Title slide has all required elements.

### Outcome Evaluation:
- Exact match on name "Jane Austen"
- Title of book present
- Photo of book cover present and valid
- Structural Location match for name at bottom of slide, below title and photo.
- Structural Location match for title above name and below photo.
- Photo is above both title and name.

## Checkpoint 2 (25 pt):
The character slides meet the requirements.

### Outcome Evaluation:
- The character chosen is among the list of major characters from the book (based on a predefined list of 27 characters with alias groups) and the character's name is the title. Character-and-alias pairs (e.g., "Jane Eyre" and "the governess", "Bertha Mason" and "the madwoman") are treated as the same character and cannot both occupy character-slide slots.
- Each character slide has a different background color.
- Each character slide has at least 3 bullet points describing their characteristics.
- Each character slide has a source link at the bottom of the slide.
- The characteristics are all direct quotes from the sources links in the slide.

## Checkpoint 3 (2 pt):
The author slide is correct.

### Outcome Evaluation:
- Exact match on author name: either "Charlotte Brontë" (diacritics required) or the pseudonym "Currer Bell" under which Jane Eyre was first published.
- Photo of author present and valid. Em or VLM match with examples.

## Checkpoint 4 (2 pt):
References slide is correct.

### Outcome Evaluation:
- At least 3 different reference links present.
- All reference links are valid URLs found in the agent trace.
